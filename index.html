Privacy Policy

Get Tag: Tag Extractor · Effective date: 1 November 2026

Get Tag: Tag Extractor ("Get Tag", "the app", "we", "us") is an Android app. This policy explains what information the app uses and why. By using the app, you agree to this policy.

1. Information we do not collect

Get Tag has no accounts and no sign-in. We do not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or location. We do not run our own servers that store your data.

2. YouTube API Services

When you paste or share a YouTube link, the app uses YouTube API Services to get that video's public details: title, description, tags, thumbnail, view, like and comment counts, and duration. The app reads only public video information. It does not ask for access to your YouTube or Google account. By using the app you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Get Tag is an independent app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by YouTube or Google. YouTube is a trademark of Google LLC.

3. History stored on your device

The app keeps a list of the last 20 videos you looked up so you can open them again, including without internet. This list is stored only on your phone. It is not sent to us. In line with YouTube API rules, saved video details are refreshed when you are online and are deleted once they are older than 30 days. You can remove this data at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

4. Advertising

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob and Google may collect and use information from your device, such as your advertising ID, IP address, device type, operating system, and app usage data, to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud. Where the law requires it, you will be asked for your consent. Learn how Google uses data from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalization in your phone's Settings under Google > Ads.

5. Downloads and sharing

When you save a thumbnail or the data file, it is stored on your phone (Pictures/GetTag and Downloads/GetTag). When you use Share, the content goes to the app you choose, and that app's own privacy policy applies. Thumbnails, titles and descriptions belong to their original creators. Use them with respect for their rights.

6. Feedback and support

The Support screen opens your email app with a message addressed to us. If you send it, we receive your email address and your message. We use them only to reply and to improve the app.

7. Children

Get Tag is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

8. Security

Data kept by the app stays on your device. Requests to YouTube are sent over an encrypted connection (HTTPS).

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
